System Limitations

  • F5 Key: Pressing this will reload your browser page and reset your tester progress.
  • PrintScreen (PrtSc): This key often will not light up because your Operating System or snipping tool intercepts the signal before the browser can see it.
  • Windows/Meta Key: Might open your start menu while testing.
